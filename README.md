# Maths Foundations — from scratch

Rebuilding the mathematical foundations of machine learning: proven on paper,
then implemented without any library. The goal is to understand the algorithms
(gradient descent, backpropagation…) from the inside — never as magic formulas.

## Why this repo
At 42 School, I've mostly learned the power of self-study.
This is why I'm trying to make my way towards AI research by studying
and building mathematical concepts applied to Machine Learning from scratch.


## Method: the 3 pillars
No concept is accepted until it holds on three axes:
1. **Vision** — the geometric intuition of what happens in space.
2. **Rigor** — a formal, general proof, with no numerical values.
3. **Code** — a from-scratch implementation (pure Python, zero dependencies)
   that acts as the final judge of the concept.
